\section{Introduction}
This document introduces the charging behaviour of a resistor-capacitor (RC) network. When a capacitor \textit{C} is charged through a series resistor \textit{R} from a constant supply $V_0$, the voltage capacitor voltage rises according to

\begin{equation}
    V(t) = V_0(1 - e^{-t/RC}),
\end{equation}

where the produce \textit{RC} has units of seconds and is called the time constant, $T = RC$.
